Federal Reserve Indicates Pause on Rate Hikes Amid Cooling Inflation Data

MACRO FED

WASHINGTON — In a move widely anticipated by financial markets, Federal Reserve officials signaled today that they are prepared to halt their aggressive campaign of interest rate increases, citing recent data that suggests inflation is steadily cooling down to the central bank's target levels.

The policy statement, released following a two-day meeting of the Federal Open Market Committee (FOMC), struck a notably less hawkish tone than previous communications. Chairman Jerome Powell is expected to elaborate on the committee's consensus during his press conference later this afternoon.

Market reactions were immediate, with major equity indices paring early losses and Treasury yields dipping slightly across the curve. The decision reflects growing confidence that the economy can achieve a "soft landing" without triggering a severe recession.
